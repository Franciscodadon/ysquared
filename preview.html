01Mission

Y Squared multiplies the potential in young people across Tampa Bay.

We teach kids to build and express themselves through science, technology, entrepreneurship, architecture, and media — and we mentor the next wave of innovators.

02Vision

A Tampa Bay where every young person finds the thing they are obsessed with building.

And the confidence to build it for a living, and for the community they came from.

Five pillars. One way of thinking.

Our S.T.E.A.M. isn’t the standard one. We swapped in the two things young people ask for most and are handed least: ownership, and the ability to make something other people see.

Four doors into the pillars. One way in for homeschool families.

Each program teaches a pillar through work students actually want to finish. The Homeschool Co-Op isn’t a fifth program — it’s all five pillars delivered in one weekly schedule. Ages 7 to 18, with the ceiling raised as they grow.

∑ All five pillars · Weekly

Homeschool Co-Op

Science. Technology. Entrepreneurship. Architecture. Media.

All five pillars in one weekly schedule. Homeschooling gives your child freedom — what it can’t always give them is a lab partner, a build team, or a teacher who specializes in the thing they’re obsessed with. Two dedicated instructors, specialty teachers, and regular field trips.

  • 01 Explorers · K–5
  • 02 Builders · 6–8
  • 03 Innovators · 9–12
¶Media · Creative writing

The Writers Room

Write it. Speak it. Own it.

Every young person has something to say — most were never taught how to say it well. Students learn the craft behind lyrical expression: cadence, wordplay, metaphor, structure, and revise until the line lands.

  • 01 Rookies · 7–10
  • 02 Session · 11–14
  • 03 Studio · 15–18
▷Media · Visual arts & AI filmmaking

Media Masters

Visual arts in the age of AI filmmaking.

Kids don’t want to draw a bowl of fruit — they want to make something people watch. Students direct their own short films, learning the real craft: composition, color, lighting, character design, and story.

  • 01 Storyboard · 7–10
  • 02 Director’s Chair · 11–14
  • 03 Studio · 15–18
⌘Technology · AI fluency

Y Squared Tech

Use it. Question it. Build with it.

AI is already in their homework, their feeds, and the jobs waiting for them. Y Squared Tech teaches students to work with it and think past it — how it decides, where it gets things wrong, and how to build something no one else could.

  • 01 Ask · 7–10
  • 02 Build · 11–14
  • 03 Deploy · 15–18
✦Science · Culinary arts

Kids in the Kitchen

Culinary arts for kids.

Kids learn to cook. They start with knife safety, measuring, and following a recipe, then work up to full meals they make on their own. Every session ends with everyone sitting down and eating what they made.

  • 01 Prep · 7–10
  • 02 Line · 11–14
  • 03 Service · 15–18

How we work

What every program has in common.

01

Built toward a career

We’re preparing kids for work, not hobbies. Every pillar leads somewhere real, and we develop students until they can do it at a level people pay for — so the thing they love becomes the thing they do.

02

Projects, not worksheets

Nothing here is graded on a page. Students plan it, build it, break it, and fix it. Skills stick when they’re attached to something a kid actually wanted to finish.

03

Trained on the real thing

Students work with the same software, equipment, and AI a working professional opened this morning. When they walk into a job, a studio, or a kitchen, none of it is unfamiliar.

It takes a room full of adults.
